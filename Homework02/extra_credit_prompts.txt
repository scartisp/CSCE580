Extra credit: prompts the model can handle
==========================================

When a prompt is given, generate_image ignores the other arguments and reads the prompt for the same four
things the model is conditioned on: digit, digit color, background and rotation. The trained network then
generates the image from those conditions. Besides the four standard backgrounds, the model was also trained
on three more USC locations (Thomas Cooper Library, Russell House, the Cocky statue) that only prompts can
ask for. Anything the prompt does not mention is chosen at random (except rotation, which defaults to nearly
upright, within 15 degrees). Words the model has no concept of (e.g.
"Cocky", "holding") are ignored, so a prompt still produces the parts it understands.

Digit
  - Numerals: "a 5", "the number 3", "#7", "digit 4"
  - Words: "zero" ... "nine" ("one" only counts if no other number appears, so "one of the students wrote
    a 7" gives a 7)
  - Multi-digit numbers ("15 students") are not read as a digit.

Digit color
  - red, blue, green, white, black
  - USC / synonym colors: garnet, crimson, scarlet, maroon, cardinal -> red; navy, azure, cobalt -> blue;
    emerald, lime -> green; ivory -> white; ebony -> black
  - If several colors appear ("garnet and black 4"), the first one is used.

Background
  - The Horseshoe: "Horseshoe", "the Shoe", "Historic Horseshoe", "Maxcy Monument", "Rutledge Chapel",
    "McKissick Museum", "President's House"
  - The CSCE 580 classroom: "classroom", "lecture hall", "our class", "in class", "CSCE 580"
  - Thomas Cooper Library: "Thomas Cooper Library", "Thomas Cooper", "Cooper Library", "TCL", "the library"
  - Russell House: "Russell House", "Russell", "student union", "the union"
  - The Cocky statue: "Cocky statue", "statue of Cocky", "Cocky's statue", "Cocky on a bench", "Gamecock statue".
    "Cocky", "mascot" or "Gamecock" on their own also mean the statue, but only when no other place is named,
    so "Cocky ... at Thomas Cooper Library" is set at the library.
  (The library, Russell House and Cocky statue backgrounds were trained from photos taken at night, so they
  look like those places at night.)
  - Plain backgrounds: "white background", "on a black backdrop", "background is white", "on plain black"
  - "black background" sets the background, not the digit color.

Rotation (positive = counter-clockwise, limited to -90..90 degrees)
  - Exact angles: "rotated 45 degrees", "tilted -30°", "45 degrees clockwise", "turned left 60 degrees",
    "at a 50 degree angle to the right"
  - Words: "tilted / leaning / slanted left|right" (30 degrees), "slightly tilted ..." (15 degrees),
    "sideways", "on its side", "lying down" (90 degrees), "upright" / "not rotated" (0 degrees)

Example prompts
  - "Cocky the mascot holding a 5 at Thomas Cooper Library"     -> a 5 at Thomas Cooper Library, random color
  - "a garnet 3 in front of Russell House"                      -> red 3, Russell House
  - "Cocky holding a blue 7"                                    -> blue 7, Cocky statue
  - "a garnet 7 on the Horseshoe tilted 30 degrees"             -> red 7, Horseshoe, 30 degrees
  - "A blue eight in our CSCE 580 classroom"                    -> blue 8, classroom
  - "the number 3 rotated 45 degrees clockwise on a black background" -> 3, black background, -45 degrees
  - "Cocky holding a garnet and black 4 in front of Rutledge Chapel"  -> red 4, Horseshoe
  - "a 6 lying on its side at the Maxcy Monument"               -> 6, Horseshoe, 90 degrees

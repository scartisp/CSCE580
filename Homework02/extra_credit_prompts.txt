Extra credit: prompts the model can handle
==========================================

When a prompt is given, generate_image ignores the other arguments and reads the prompt for five things the
image network is conditioned on: digit, digit color, background, rotation, and whether Cocky is in the picture
holding the digit. The trained diffusion network then generates the image from those conditions.

How the prompt is read
  1. Keywords: exact phrases for numbers, colors, places, angles and the mascot (lists below).
  2. A pretrained sentence encoder (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, a 6-layer BERT), re-implemented as a PyTorch network in
     model_def.py with its weights and vocabulary stored in the .pt file, so it runs in the class environment
     with no extra packages. When the keywords find no place or no mascot, it compares short spans of the
     prompt with descriptions of each place and of Cocky, and uses the closest one if it is similar enough.
     So "a 5 near the big library on campus", "a 3 where students grab lunch" or "the USC rooster holding a 4"
     work even though they use none of the keywords. Unknown places (e.g. "Williams-Brice Stadium", "the State
     House") are not forced onto a background; a random one is used instead.

Anything the prompt does not mention is chosen at random (except rotation, which defaults to nearly upright,
within 15 degrees).

What the model can draw
  - Digits 0-9 (MNIST handwriting) in red, blue, green, white or black, rotated -90..90 degrees.
  - Backgrounds: plain white, plain black, the CSCE 580 classroom, the Horseshoe, and three more USC places
    trained from my own photos: Thomas Cooper Library, Russell House and the Cocky statue (these three were
    photographed at night, so they look like those places at night).
  - Cocky the mascot holding the digit: a small cartoon Cocky
    standing on one side of the image with the digit, made smaller, next to his wing. It can appear on any
    background, so "Cocky the mascot holding a 5 at Thomas Cooper Library" draws exactly that.

Digit
  - Numerals: "a 5", "the number 3", "#7", "digit 4"
  - Words: "zero" ... "nine" ("one" only counts if no other number appears, so "one of the students wrote
    a 7" gives a 7)
  - Multi-digit numbers ("15 students") are not read as a digit.

Digit color
  - red, blue, green, white, black
  - USC / synonym colors: garnet, crimson, scarlet, maroon, cardinal -> red; navy, azure, cobalt -> blue;
    emerald, lime -> green; ivory -> white; ebony -> black
  - If several colors appear ("garnet and black 4"), the first one is used.

Background
  - The Horseshoe: "Horseshoe", "the Shoe", "Historic Horseshoe", "Maxcy Monument", "Rutledge Chapel",
    "McKissick Museum", "President's House"; by meaning: "the lawn under the old oak trees", "the oldest part
    of campus"
  - The CSCE 580 classroom: "classroom", "lecture hall", "our class", "in class", "CSCE 580"; by meaning:
    "where students take notes at their desks"
  - Thomas Cooper Library: "Thomas Cooper Library", "Thomas Cooper", "Cooper Library", "TCL", "the library";
    by meaning: "the big library on campus", "the place where you check out books"
  - Russell House: "Russell House", "Russell", "student union", "the union"; by meaning: "the student center",
    "where students grab lunch"
  - The Cocky statue: "Cocky statue", "statue of Cocky", "Cocky's statue", "Gamecock statue"; by meaning:
    "the bronze statue on a bench"
  - Plain backgrounds: "white background", "on a black backdrop", "background is white", "on plain black"
  - "black background" sets the background, not the digit color.

Cocky holding the digit
  - "Cocky", "mascot", "Gamecock(s)"; by meaning: "the USC rooster", "a gamecock bird", "a cartoon rooster",
    "our school mascot"
  - "the Cocky statue" means the statue background instead (the mascot is not drawn unless also named).

Rotation (positive = counter-clockwise, limited to -90..90 degrees)
  - Exact angles: "rotated 45 degrees", "tilted -30°", "45 degrees clockwise", "turned left 60 degrees",
    "at a 50 degree angle to the right"
  - Words: "tilted / leaning / slanted left|right" (30 degrees), "slightly tilted ..." (15 degrees),
    "sideways", "on its side", "lying down" (90 degrees), "upright" / "not rotated" (0 degrees)

Example prompts
  - "Cocky the mascot holding a 5 at Thomas Cooper Library"     -> Cocky holding a 5, Thomas Cooper Library
  - "the USC rooster holding a 4 near the big library on campus" -> Cocky holding a 4, Thomas Cooper Library
  - "Cocky holding a blue 7"                                    -> Cocky holding a blue 7, random background
  - "a garnet 3 in front of Russell House"                      -> red 3, Russell House
  - "a green 6 where students grab lunch, tilted left"          -> green 6, Russell House, 30 degrees
  - "a 2 next to the Cocky statue"                              -> 2, Cocky statue
  - "a garnet 7 on the Horseshoe tilted 30 degrees"             -> red 7, Horseshoe, 30 degrees
  - "A blue eight in our CSCE 580 classroom"                    -> blue 8, classroom
  - "the number 3 rotated 45 degrees clockwise on a black background" -> 3, black background, -45 degrees
  - "a 6 lying on its side at the Maxcy Monument"               -> 6, Horseshoe, 90 degrees
